## About

I’m Brad Josh Nebreja, an aspiring full-stack developer and UI/UX designer based in Calamba City, Laguna, Philippines. I turn complex ideas into clear, useful digital experiences, with attention to how websites look, function and help people get things done.

I’m currently developing **PraisePoint**, a church presentation workspace. It is my work in progress for 2026 and remains under development. I’m also looking for an internship where I can contribute to interface design, web development and quality assurance.
